The big change

Before this commit, students watched videos one by one from the Videos page. Now content is grouped into lectures: one lecture holds its videos, its exams and its PDF files together, the way a teacher actually teaches a class.

In the site header, الفيديوهات was replaced by المحاضرات, which opens /lectures. The old Videos page still exists and still works.

The commit also added prerequisite locks, lecture availability windows, lecture reports, and a curriculum manager for package courses. Each has its own section below.

A lecture is set for one grade, or left open to all grades. The same video or exam can be added to more than one lecture; it is linked, not copied.

  • Standalone lectures and chapters are free content for the grade. Every student of that grade sees them.
  • Package lectures are visible only to students with an approved purchase of that package.
3

The lectures page

Address: /lectures. One page, with different tools for teachers and students.

Student
  • Sees three sections for their grade: standalone lectures, الدروس والوحدات الدراسية (chapters), and their purchased packages.
  • دخول المحاضرة opens the lecture workspace: the video player, the list of the lecture's videos, its exams and its PDF files.
  • Opening an exam from a lecture shows العودة للمحاضرة to come back to it.
  • Locked items show a lock icon. Clicking one explains which exam to pass first.
Teacher
  • Picks the grade at the top, then creates a lecture or a chapter.
  • The create form adds videos, exams and PDF files in one step, and can mark an exam as the key to one of the lecture's videos.
  • Each lecture card has tools to attach an existing video or exam, upload a PDF, set availability, archive, edit and delete.
  • A toggle shows archived lectures.
Who
Teachers (admins) and assistants with the videos permission manage lectures. The page is switched on per platform with the lectures feature in the Super Admin panel.
4

Curriculum manager

Address: control panel → إدارة المحاضرات (/control-panel/curriculum). It is the place to build package courses. The packages panel links to it with إدارة الفصول والمحاضرات (المنهج).

  • Choose a package, then add chapters (إضافة فصل دراسي جديد) and lectures inside them.
  • Link videos and exams to a lecture, and upload its PDF notes (رفع مذكرة / ملف PDF للمحاضرة).
  • Set a lecture's lock: تحديد شرط الفتح التلقائي للمحاضرة.
  • مستودع المحتوى غير المرتبط بمحاضرات lists every video and exam that is not in any lecture yet, so nothing gets forgotten.

Only people with the videos or payments permission can open it.

5

Prerequisite locks

A teacher can make content open only after the student passes an exam. A whole lecture, one video or one exam can be locked this way.

Example
  1. The teacher sets: Lecture 2 opens after scoring 80% on the Chapter 1 exam.
  2. A student with a best score of 55% sees Lecture 2 locked, with the exam's name, the 80% target and their own score.
  3. The student retakes the exam and scores 85%. The result screen shows the pass, and Lecture 2 opens straight away, without reloading the page.
  • Setting the score: النسبة المئوية المطلوبة للاجتياز (%), default 70%. The teacher can change it at any time.
  • Which attempt counts: the student's best submitted attempt. Pre-video quizzes don't count.
  • Loops are blocked: the database refuses a rule that would make two items wait on each other forever.
  • Who is locked: students only. Teachers and assistants always see everything open.

When the commit landed, these locks were enforced only on the page. Since the fixes of 24 Sep, the database and the file and video servers check them too, so a link can't get around them. See section 10.

6

Availability and archive

  • Availability window: a lecture can stay open for a number of hours after it is published, or until a set date. The card shows the time left, for example متبقي 3 أيام, then انتهت الإتاحة.
  • Extensions: the teacher can extend or close a lecture for one student, one group or the whole grade. The most specific setting wins: student, then group, then grade.
  • Archive: أرشفة المحاضرة hides a lecture from students without deleting anything. إلغاء أرشفة المحاضرة brings it back.
7

Lecture PDF files

Each lecture can carry PDF notes. When a student taps a file, the server checks that they are signed in, on an allowed device, in the right grade, have bought the package if there is one, and that the lecture is unlocked, open and not archived. Only then does it give a download link, which works for 5 minutes.

Files are stored in Cloudflare R2. A private storage bucket for them is ready in the code and turns on once it is created (see section 11).

9

What happened to old content

  • Nothing was deleted. Every existing video, exam, attempt and watch-progress record stayed as it was.
  • Playlists that belonged to a package became chapters of that package.
  • 13 specific older videos were each placed in their own standalone lecture.
  • Videos and exams that are in no lecture stay usable as before and appear in the curriculum manager's unassigned list.
  • No lock rules were created automatically. Every lock is set by a teacher.
